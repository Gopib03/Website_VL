What sets our platform apart

Three pillars of a USFDA-ready sterile line

Every design decision in our injectable platform — from cleanroom layout to data integrity — is made against USFDA expectations, not retrofitted to meet them later.

Isolator and RABS-based filling, closed-system transfers, and media-fill qualified operators — built to minimize human intervention in the Grade A zone.

  • Isolator & RABS-based aseptic filling
  • Closed-system material and component transfer
  • Media-fill qualified operators and gowning program

Facility, utilities, and quality systems designed to 21 CFR Parts 210/211 and Annex 1, with data integrity and CAPA processes built for FDA pre-approval inspection.

  • Facility & utilities designed to 21 CFR Parts 210/211
  • Data integrity controls aligned to ALCOA+ principles
  • CAPA and deviation management built for FDA PAI

Fully validated filling, lyophilization, and inspection lines that scale from clinical batches to commercial volumes without re-qualification surprises.

  • IQ/OQ/PQ validated filling and lyophilization
  • Automated visual inspection and serialization
  • Scales clinical to commercial without re-validation risk

SynVaultLine™ — Sterile Platform

One aseptic core, engineered against 21 CFR 211 and Annex 1 from the ground up

SynVaultLine integrates isolator-based aseptic filling, automated visual inspection, and serialization into a single continuous run. Environmental monitoring, media fills, and batch records are built for USFDA pre-approval inspection from the outset — not layered on after commissioning — with full electronic traceability from raw material to shipping case.

Why US-Based Manufacturing Wins — 4 Strategic Pillars

Regulatory Advantage
  • Built fully within US FDA jurisdiction
  • Lower inspection volatility vs offshore
  • Compliance-by-design (no retrofits)
  • Faster ANDA commercialization post qualification
  • Reduced long-term regulatory risk
Strategic & Commercial Advantage
  • Strong demand for US domestic sterile capacity
